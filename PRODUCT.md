## Platform

## Stack

Next.js (App Router) with TypeScript, React, Tailwind CSS, lucide-react icons, and Next Image. Pinned by the client brief. Deploys to Vercel. Contact form delivers through the Resend email API from a server action (RESEND_API_KEY in Vercel env).

## Users

- Homeowners in Bucks County, Montgomery County, and Philadelphia, PA with a plumbing, drain, water heater, or heating problem. Often stressed, sometimes mid-emergency (backed-up sewer, no hot water, leak). Usually on a phone. Job: decide quickly whether this plumber is trustworthy and reachable, then call.
- Light commercial property managers and owners (small offices, retail, multi-unit buildings, facilities needing backflow testing). Job: find a licensed Master Plumber who responds fast and keeps disruption low.

Marketing site for Brett Sabol Plumbing, Heating & Jet Drain Cleaning, a family-owned plumbing company based in Feasterville-Trevose, PA. Replaces a dated Duda template site at www.brettsabolplumbing.com. Success means more phone calls and quote requests from the service area, and better organic visibility for plumbing, jet drain cleaning, heating, and backflow searches in the three service areas.

## Product Principles

1. The phone number is the product. Every screen offers a one-tap call without hunting.
2. Prove with the client's own trucks, crew, and job sites. Never borrow stock or invent proof.
3. Credentials are specific: Master Plumber Lic. #43391 and Backflow Certified, stated plainly, not inflated.
4. Speak to a stressed homeowner and a busy property manager in plain language, with honest next steps.
5. Family-owned and owner-led is a fact to show, not a slogan to repeat.
